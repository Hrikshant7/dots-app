Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Dots does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Data on your device

Your scores, stars, streaks and Alertness Scores are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us, and they are deleted if you delete the app.

Game Center

Leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, your best scores and your Game Center nickname are shared with Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

No tracking

Dots contains no advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind.

Children

Dots is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
