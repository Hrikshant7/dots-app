Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email thrikshant@gmail.com. I usually reply within two days.

How is the Alertness Score calculated?

From four parts of the 60-second Daily Check: how quickly your correct taps come (35%), accuracy (30%), whether you keep your pace in the second half (20%) and lives kept (15%). It's simple, open math for fun and self-tracking, not a medical measurement.

How do I unlock Levels 3–6?

Tap any locked level and choose Unlock all levels. It's a one-time purchase: no subscription, no ads.

I bought the full game. How do I get it on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, tap any locked level, then tap Restore Purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Leaderboards say they aren't available

Leaderboards use Game Center. Sign in under Settings → Game Center on your iPhone, then reopen Dots.

Does my streak reset if I miss a day?

Your streak counts days in a row with a Daily Check. You have until midnight to keep it going.
